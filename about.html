Our story

Built by golfers who guide.

Safari first, golf woven through. We started because the trips we wanted to take did not exist: real safari, real golf, and no one to stitch the two together properly.

How it began

Two halves of a day, and a lifetime in Africa.

The first golf safari we ran was for ourselves: a round at Karen on the way to the Mara, because the tee time was there and the flight was not until four. The day had two halves, and both were better for the other.

Since then we have played and slept our way across thirteen countries, walked every course we sell and stayed in every camp. We know which guide to ask for at which lodge, which fairways flood in April, and which border posts to avoid on a Friday. That is the whole of the business: knowing, and then arranging.

We remain small on purpose. Hosted departures never carry more than eight golfers. Tailor-made journeys are handled by one named planner from the first call to the last transfer, and by a local host on WhatsApp for every hour in between.

What we believe

Four rules we do not break.

01

Safari first

The wild sets the calendar. We fit the golf around migration dates, dry seasons and the best guides' availability, never the other way round.

02

Only courses worth the flight

If we would not fly for it, we do not sell it. Every course on a journey has been played by us in the last two seasons.

03

Non-golfers travel as equals

Every golf morning has a safari, spa or vineyard alternative, and the two halves of the group meet for lunch.

04

One per cent goes back

One per cent of every journey supports the conservancies and communities that host us. We will show you the work, not just the logo.
