Good to know

Questions we're asked most.

Do I need to bring my own clubs?

Bring them if you can. Every course we use has quality rental sets, and we can arrange a single set to travel with you between courses so you never carry them on internal flights.

What handicap do I need?

Most clubs ask for a certificate of 28 or better for men and 36 for women. Beginners are welcome on the shorter journeys, and non-golfing partners join the safari and spa days at a reduced rate.

When is the best time to travel?

The dry season from June to October is best for game viewing and golf across most of the region. Kenya and Tanzania are also excellent from January to March. Each journey lists its best months.

What is included in the price?

Accommodation, most meals, green fees and caddies where customary, park and conservancy fees, game drives, internal flights and all transfers. International flights, visas, travel insurance, drinks at some lodges and gratuities are excluded.

How much does a golf safari cost?

Journeys start at around US$3,600 per person sharing for a five-night short break and run to US$18,500 and above for a fourteen-night, three-country crossing. Most guests travel between US$6,000 and US$9,000.

Can non-golfers come?

Yes, and they usually have the better week. Every golf morning has a safari, spa or vineyard alternative, and the group meets again for lunch.

Is my money protected?

Deposits are held in a client trust account and every journey is covered by our travel-operator bond. We'll send the details with your first draft.
