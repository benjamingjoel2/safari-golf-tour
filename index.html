What we arrange

The best of golf, safari, stays and encounters.

The courses

Tee times, caddies, club hire and transfers at every course on the journey. Your handicap certificate lodged before you arrive.

The camps

Owner-run camps and lodges in private conservancies, chosen for their guides first and their pillows second. We have slept in every one.

The encounters

A round with a touring professional, an hour with a gorilla family, a morning with the rangers who protect the rhino. Arranged quietly, in advance.

Scheduled light aircraft between camps, private charter where it saves a day, and clubs that fly with you rather than after you.

Why a golf safari

The bush empties you out. The game fills you back up.

Dawn belongs to the wild: the drives, the silence, the day's first cat. By late morning you are on the tee with the light behind you. Golfers and non-golfers travel together and come home equally full.

Golf · South Africa

The Links after the refurbishment

Fancourt's flagship reopened with new bunkering and a firmer, faster surface. It plays two shots harder into the wind and is better for it.

Safari · Kenya

When to see the Mara crossings

The herds have been arriving earlier each year. Late July into September is now the safest window, with the conservancies quietest in the second half.

Safari · Botswana

Chobe by boat, not by road

In the dry season the riverfront is the whole show. We now put every Chobe afternoon on the water, and keep the 4x4 for dawn.
