FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to bring my own clubs?

Bring them if you can. Every course we use has quality rental sets, and we can arrange a set to travel with you between courses so you never carry them on internal flights.

What handicap do I need?

Most clubs ask for a handicap certificate of 28 or better for men and 36 for women. Beginners are welcome on our shorter packages, and non-golfing partners can join the safari and spa days at a reduced rate.

When is the best time to travel?

The dry season from June to October is best for both game viewing and golf across most of the region. Kenya and Tanzania are also excellent from January to March. Each package lists its best months.

Are the prices all-inclusive?

Prices include accommodation, most meals, green fees, caddies where customary, park and conservancy fees, game drives, internal flights and all transfers. They exclude international flights, visas, travel insurance, drinks at some lodges and gratuities.

Can you build a custom itinerary?

Yes. Every package on this page is a starting point. Tell us your dates and wish list through the form below and we'll tailor it.
