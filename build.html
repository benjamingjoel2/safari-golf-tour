      About the price

      Indicative, and honest about it.

      The builder's price uses our average rates per course day and per park night, plus a transfer allowance for each stop and each border. It excludes international flights, visas, insurance and gratuities. Your planner's proposal replaces it with real camp rates for your dates, which usually land within fifteen per cent.
