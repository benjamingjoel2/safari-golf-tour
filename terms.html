Terms & conditions.

The terms on which we take bookings. A draft for review before launch; the version you sign is the one sent with your confirmation.

1. The contract

A booking is confirmed when we have received your signed booking form and deposit and have issued a written confirmation. The contract is between you and Safari Golf Tour for the services described in your confirmed itinerary. International flights are not part of the contract unless we have booked them for you and said so in writing.

2. Payment

A deposit of 25% of the journey price is payable on booking. The balance is due 70 days before departure. For bookings made within 70 days of departure the full price is payable on booking. Prices are quoted in US dollars, per person sharing, and are guaranteed once the deposit has been received, except for increases in park fees, conservancy fees or government taxes imposed after confirmation, which are passed on at cost.

3. Changes by you

We will try to accommodate changes to a confirmed itinerary. Changes are subject to availability and to any costs charged by camps, courses and airlines, plus an amendment fee of US$150 per booking. A change of departure date within 70 days of travel is treated as a cancellation.

4. Cancellation by you

Cancellation must be in writing. Charges are a percentage of the journey price: more than 70 days before departure, the deposit; 70 to 43 days, 50%; 42 to 15 days, 75%; 14 days or fewer, 100%. Gorilla permits, hosted-departure places and tournament-week tee times are non-refundable once secured, as noted on your confirmation.

5. Changes and cancellation by us

Occasionally we must change an element of a journey, for instance when a camp closes for weather or a course closes for a tournament. We will offer an alternative of equivalent standard, or a refund of any difference. If we cancel a journey for reasons within our control, you may choose a full refund or an alternative journey. Hosted departures require a minimum of four golfers; if that number is not reached 90 days before departure we will offer a private departure at the tailor-made price, a later date, or a full refund.

6. Insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance covering cancellation, medical expenses, emergency evacuation and personal belongings, including golf equipment, is a condition of booking. We will ask for the policy details before departure.

7. Health, visas and documents

You are responsible for passports, visas, vaccinations and any medication for the countries visited. We provide current guidance with your confirmation, and again 60 days before departure, but requirements change and the final check is yours.

8. Golf

Most clubs require a handicap certificate of 28 or better for men and 36 for women. Dress codes apply and are set out in your journey notes. Tee times are confirmed but may be moved by the club for weather, maintenance or tournaments, in which case we arrange the nearest alternative.

9. Safari and responsibility

Safari involves proximity to wild animals, light aircraft, and remote areas. You agree to follow the instructions of guides, rangers and pilots at all times. We select camps, guides and operators with care, but we do not own or operate them, and our liability for their acts or omissions is limited to the extent permitted by law.

10. Financial protection

Deposits and balances are held in a client trust account until the services have been provided. Details of our operator bond are provided with every confirmation.

11. Complaints

If something is not right while you are travelling, tell your host or planner immediately so it can be put right on the spot. Any unresolved complaint should be sent to us in writing within 28 days of your return.

12. Law

These terms are governed by the law of the country in which the booking office named on your confirmation is located, and disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
