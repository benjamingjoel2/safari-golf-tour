Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and what we do with it. A draft for review before launch.

What we collect

When you enquire we collect your name, email address and, if you give them, your phone number, country, travel dates, group details and golf handicap. When you book we also collect passport details, dietary and medical information relevant to travel, insurance details and emergency contacts, because camps, airlines and park authorities require them.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to design and book your journey, to lodge handicap certificates with clubs, to secure permits and tee times, and to look after you while you travel. We also use your contact details to send you our occasional journal and departure news, which you can stop at any time by replying or using the link in any email.

Who we share it with

Only the suppliers who need it to deliver your journey: camps and hotels, golf clubs, airlines and charter operators, ground handlers, park and permit authorities, and insurers where you ask us to arrange cover. Some of these are outside your home country. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details for two years from our last contact. Booking records for seven years, as tax and financial-protection rules require. Passport and medical details are deleted 90 days after your journey ends unless you ask us to keep them for a future booking.

Cookies and this website

This website sets no tracking cookies. If you use the enquiry form, your draft is held in your own browser only until it is sent. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and photography from Unsplash, and those services may record the request in their own logs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, subject to the retention rules above. Write to us at the address in the footer and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy here and, where the change is material, tell existing guests by email.
